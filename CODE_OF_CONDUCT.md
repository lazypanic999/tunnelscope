# Code of Conduct

TunnelScope welcomes people with different backgrounds and experience levels.
Be respectful, assume good intent, and keep feedback focused on ideas and
evidence. Harassment, threats, discrimination, and sharing someone else's
private information are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may edit or remove comments and contributions that violate
these expectations, or restrict participation when necessary. To report a
conduct concern, open an issue requesting a private contact channel without
posting names or personal details. The maintainer will arrange a private way to
receive the report and review it as discreetly as possible.
