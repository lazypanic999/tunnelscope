# TunnelScope v0.9.1 (unreleased)

Development work after [v0.9.0](v0.9.0.md). The published package remains
v0.9.0 until a matching release tag and distribution are available.

## Reject malformed report kinds cleanly

List/object `kind` metadata no longer causes an uncaught TypeError in text
`show` before the normal report validator runs. The CLI exits with a concise
error, leaves the input unchanged and creates no output report. Valid and
legacy producer-version display is unchanged. Thanks to
[sunxiuguo](https://github.com/sunxiuguo) for
[#53](https://github.com/lazypanic999/tunnelscope/pull/53).

## Preserve uncertainty in partial receiver captures

A receiver records arrival before sending its reply. If it is interrupted
during that send, the saved capture can contain a valid arrival with no
`ack_send_status`. Previously, joining this capture with a timed-out sized
probe could label the reply as sent-but-missing (or as a sent small reply).

The join now uses `return_evidence: receiver_reply_send_unverified` when the
local send outcome is missing or unknown. It counts these separately in
correlations and joined diagnoses, and text, HTML and Markdown keep them
visible. Known accepted sends, explicit socket rejections and replies
recorded by the sender retain their existing cases.

The interpretation also describes accepted local sends and the sender's
deadline rather than claiming a reply never arrived or identifying its loss
location. Text summaries associate timeouts with receiver arrivals without
asserting their cross-host chronological order. Schema version 1 and the UDP protocol are unchanged; the new field
and evidence case are additive.

Validation includes interrupting the receiver's send call, reading its saved
partial capture and joining it, unknown small/sized send results, rendering,
and aggregation across three diagnosis runs. No VM or external network is
required for those regressions.
